# Lite Public-Targets Study - what the data can and cannot say (2026-10-13)
**Sample:** 55 hand-selected US public technology targets, 2015-2025 (NOT a systematic universe; selection toward large, well-known deals). EV/Revenue sample N=35; takeover-premium sample N=20.
**AI labels:** two independent coders (kappa 0.77 binary strict, weighted kappa 0.91), one disagreement (Verint) adjudicated by the project owner; 4-row re-audit performed with GPT (D-024). Only 18 pooled companies were coded; the other targets are assumed non-AI (D-010). 3 strict-AI targets (Nuance, PROS, Verint), 16 broad-AI (intensity >= 1).

## Phase classification
**NOT ASSESSABLE.** Strict AI in the EV/Revenue sample: 2 deals (Nuance, Verint). Both lack prior-year revenue, so revenue growth (a required control) is missing and the complete-case model sample contains 0 strict-AI deals. The Phase 5 models were therefore not fitted, Phase 6 returned NOT ASSESSABLE, and Phase 7 carries that forward. No regression, matching or placebo result exists.

## Descriptive facts only (unadjusted; no inference)
| Measure | Broad AI (n) | Non-AI (n) | MWU p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median EV/Revenue | 7.5x (11) | 8.0x (24) | 0.47 |
| Median 1-day takeover premium | 35.2% (4) | 27.7% (16) | 0.62 |
| Median revenue growth | 16.7% (5) | 25.5% (16) | 0.28 |
| Median EBITDA margin | 1.6% (10) | 0.4% (23) | 0.62 |
Whole sample median EV/Revenue 8.0x (range 2.2x-36.1x). Differences are small and statistically indistinguishable from zero; with n of 4-11 in the AI group no conclusion about an AI premium is supported in either direction.

## What would change this
1. More strict-AI targets (need >= 30 with growth data); 2. prior-year revenue for Nuance, Verint and others; 3. a systematic universe from Capital IQ/PitchBook; 4. human audit sample; 5. unaffected prices for more public deals.
